Privacy Policy for Water Sort

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Water Sort ("the app") is a water sort puzzle game. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

No Data Collection

Water Sort does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your game progress (current level and selected bottle shape) is stored locally on your device only and is never sent anywhere.

No Internet Access

The app does not use an internet connection. It works fully offline.

No Ads, No In-App Purchases, No Third-Party Services

Water Sort contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, and no third-party tracking services.

Data Deletion

Since all data stays on your device, uninstalling the app permanently removes all your data.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. It does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page.
