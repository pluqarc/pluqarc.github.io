Privacy Policy for Drachsalf

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Drachsalf ("the app") is a flashcard study app. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

No Data Collection

Drachsalf does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All decks, flashcards, and progress you create are stored locally on your device only and are never sent anywhere.

No Internet Access

The app does not use an internet connection. It works fully offline.

No Ads, No In-App Purchases, No Third-Party Services

Drachsalf contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

Data Deletion

Since all data stays on your device, uninstalling the app permanently removes all your data.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page.
