Privacy Policy for Color the Maze

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Color the Maze ("the app") is a puzzle game for players of all ages, including children and families. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

No Data Collection

Color the Maze does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to anyone else. Your game progress, selected theme, and settings are saved locally on your device only and are never sent anywhere.

Works Offline

The app does not need an internet connection to play, and it sends no data to us or to third parties. If you choose to rate the game, the standard Google Play review window may appear. It is provided and handled by Google Play, and we receive no information from it.

No Ads, No In-App Purchases, No Third-Party Services

Color the Maze contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no user accounts, no social features, and no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.

Data Deletion

Since all data stays on your device, uninstalling the app permanently removes all your data.

Children's Privacy

Color the Maze is suitable for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. It does not ask for names, emails, locations, or any other personal details, and it contains no links that take children out of the app.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page.
