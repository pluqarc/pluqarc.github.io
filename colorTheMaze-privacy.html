Privacy Policy for Color the Maze

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Color the Maze ("the app") is a puzzle game. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

No Data Collection

Color the Maze does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your game progress, selected theme, and settings are saved locally on your device only and are never sent anywhere.

No Internet Access

The app does not use an internet connection. It works fully offline.

No Ads, No In-App Purchases, No Third-Party Services

Color the Maze contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

Data Deletion

Since all data stays on your device, uninstalling the app permanently removes all your data.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page.
