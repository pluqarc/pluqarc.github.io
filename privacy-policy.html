Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 16, 2026

Developer Information

This privacy policy applies to the Maze Rise ("Game" or "App") developed and published by Pluq Arc, an independent developer based in Tunisia.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Game available through the Google Play Store.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Local Game Data

Our Game stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Game progress (levels completed, current level)
  • Game settings and preferences

Important: If you sign in with Google Play Games, your progress is also synced to Google's servers — see Section 1.3 below.

1.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do NOT collect:

  • We do not collect your name or email
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or photos
  • We do not collect personal information for any purpose
  • We do not display advertisements
  • We do not process in-app purchases

1.3 Google Play Games Services

Our Game uses Google Play Games Services to back up and sync your game progress (unlocked level) across devices when you are signed in with your Google Play Games account.

  • Your Google Play Games account ID is used to identify your save data
  • Your unlocked level progress is stored on Google's servers and synced to your device

This data is managed by Google and subject to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can manage or delete this data through your Google Play Games account settings.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

  • Game Progress: To save your progress and continue where you left off, and to sync it across your devices via Google Play Games Services
  • Game Settings: To remember your preferences (sound settings, etc.)

Local data stays on your device. Progress data synced via Play Games Services is stored by Google as described above.

3. DATA SHARING

We do not share any data with third parties.

The external services involved are the Google Play Store for app distribution and Google Play Games Services for cloud save of your progress.

4. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

While our Game is not specifically designed or directed at children, we are committed to protecting the privacy of all users, including any children who may use the Game:

  • We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any users
  • We do not require users to provide any personal information to play the Game
  • All game data is stored locally on the device only
  • The Game does not contain social features, chat, or user-generated content
  • The Game does not contain links to websites or social media

4.1 COPPA Compliance (US Users)

Our Game complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

4.2 GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

Our Game complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no processing of personal information.

4.3 Parental Guidance

We recommend that parents and guardians supervise their children's use of mobile devices and applications.

5. DATA SECURITY

Since all game data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to any servers:

  • Your game data is as secure as your device
  • We recommend using device security features (lock screen, encryption)
  • No data can be intercepted during transmission because nothing is transmitted

6. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

All game data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all game data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the Game from your device, or
  • Clearing the Game's data through your device settings (Settings → Apps → Maze Rise → Clear Data)

This will permanently delete all your progress and settings.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Since we do not collect or store any personal information:

  • There is no personal data to access, correct, or delete from our side
  • All your game data is under your control on your device
  • You can delete your data at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing app data

8. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

We do not directly collect or transmit any data. Progress data synced via Google Play Games Services may be transferred and stored on Google's servers, which may be located outside your country, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

9. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Store: For app distribution
  • Google Play Games Services: For cloud save/sync of your game progress

Third-Party Privacy Policy:

  • Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy

10. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

If we make any significant changes (such as adding data collection or third-party services), we will notify users through the Game or the app store listing before the changes take effect.

The most current version of this Privacy Policy will always be available at: https://pluqarc.github.io/privacy-policy.html
