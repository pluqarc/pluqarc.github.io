Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 7, 2026

Developer Information

This privacy policy applies to the Maze Rise ("Game" or "App") developed and published by Pluq Arc, an independent developer based in Tunisia.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Game available through the Google Play Store.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Local Game Data

Our Game stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Game progress (levels completed, current level)
  • Endless Mode high scores
  • Purchase status (Endless Mode unlock)
  • Game settings and preferences

Important: This basic information is stored locally on your device. We also use Google Play Games Services to sync some game data across your devices (see section 1.4 below).

1.2 In-App Purchases

When you make in-app purchases (Endless Mode unlock), the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Your payment information
  • Credit card details
  • Billing addresses
  • Any personal financial data

Google Play handles all payment processing. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information about how Google handles purchase data.

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do NOT collect:

  • We do not collect your name or email directly (Google Play Games may share your public profile if you choose)
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services
  • We do not collect location data (precise or approximate)
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or photos
  • We do not collect any personal information
  • We do not use advertising or ad networks
  • We do not share any data with third parties

1.4 Google Play Games Services

When you use leaderboards, achievements, or cloud save features, we collect and share data with Google Play Games Services:

  • Your Google Play Games player ID
  • Your game scores and achievements
  • Your game progress (for cloud sync across devices)
  • Your public profile information (username, avatar - if you choose to share it)
  • Your leaderboard rankings and scores (visible to other players)

This data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can control your Google Play Games privacy settings through your Google account.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Since all game data is stored locally on your device:

  • Game Progress: To save your progress and continue where you left off
  • Endless Mode Score: To track your highest score in endless mode
  • Game Settings: To remember your preferences (sound settings, etc.)

All of this data remains on your device and is never sent anywhere.

3. DATA SHARING

We do not share any data with third parties.

The only external service involved is Google Play Store for processing in-app purchases, which is managed entirely by Google according to their own privacy policy.

4. ADVERTISING

Our Game does not display any advertisements.

We provide a completely ad-free experience for all users, including children.

5. IN-APP PURCHASES

The Game includes an optional in-app purchase (Endless Mode unlock). All payment processing is handled exclusively by the Google Play Store.

  • We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information
  • All purchase transactions are managed through your Google Play account
  • Purchases are governed by Google Play's terms and policies
  • You can set up parental controls in your Google Play settings to restrict purchases

For information about managing in-app purchases and setting up parental controls, please visit: Google Play Parental Controls

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our Game is designed to be safe for children of all ages. We are committed to protecting children's privacy:

  • We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any users
  • We do not require users to provide any personal information to play the Game
  • All game data is stored locally on the device only
  • We do not display any advertisements
  • We do not use any third-party services that collect data
  • The Game does not contain social features, chat, or user-generated content
  • The Game does not contain links to websites or social media

6.1 COPPA Compliance (US Users)

Our Game complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not collect personal information from children under 13.

6.2 GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

Our Game complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no processing of personal information.

6.3 Parental Control

Parents and guardians can control in-app purchases through Google Play parental controls. We recommend that parents:

  • Set up a PIN for purchases in Google Play settings
  • Review in-app purchase settings in Google Play
  • Monitor your child's device usage

7. DATA SECURITY

Since all game data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to any servers:

  • Your game data is as secure as your device
  • We recommend using device security features (lock screen, encryption)
  • No data can be intercepted during transmission because nothing is transmitted

8. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

All game data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all game data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the Game from your device, or
  • Clearing the Game's data through your device settings (Settings → Apps → Maze Rise → Clear Data)

This will permanently delete all your progress, endless mode scores, and settings. Note that cloud-saved data (via Google Play Games) may persist until manually deleted through Google Play Games settings.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

Since we do not collect or store any personal information:

  • There is no personal data to access, correct, or delete from our side
  • All your game data is under your control on your device
  • You can delete your data at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing app data

10. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Since we do not collect or transmit any data, there are no international data transfers. All game data remains on your device in your country.

11. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Store: For app distribution and in-app purchase processing
  • Google Play Games Services: For leaderboards, achievements, and cloud saves
  • Google Play Billing: For processing in-app purchases (Endless Mode unlock)

Google Play's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

12. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

If we make any significant changes (such as adding data collection or third-party services), we will notify users through the Game or the app store listing before the changes take effect.

The most current version of this Privacy Policy will always be available at: https://pluqarc.github.io/privacy-policy.html
